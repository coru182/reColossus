reColossus (PPSA99071): Shadow of the Colossus (PS2, USA) statically recompiled for the PS5

An unofficial fan project, not affiliated with or endorsed by Sony Interactive Entertainment or Team Ico. It contains
none of the game's files: they are read from the player's own disc image. The game's program in it is recompiled from
the USA release's boot ELF (SCUS_974.72) and its code modules (KERNEL.XFF, MANAGER.XFF, GAMECORE.XFF).

What is in the app, and under which terms

- reColossus's own code, the PS5 host (ps5/: the app's entry, the first start, the raylib subset on VideoOut, ScePad
  and SceAudioOut) and its changes to Celosia: GPL-3.0 (reColossus-GPL-3.0.txt). The first start's screen, the
  program's entry and the C library replacements come from reKing (GPL-3.0), which adapted them from reUndr-ps5's
  PS5 host by bystritskiy (MIT, reUndr-MIT.txt).
- Celosia by HenryKun55 (the Shadow of the Colossus port: its pipeline, runtime changes, native renderer and
  hand-written functions), in GenissonEmilio's Linux port of it (ShadowOfTheColossusPC), and PS2Recomp by ran-j and its contributors (the recompiler and runtime): GPL-3.0
  (reColossus-GPL-3.0.txt).
- raylib's default font (zlib/libpng licence, raylib-zlib.txt), zlib (zlib licence, zlib.txt).
- RADV from Mesa (MIT and others, Mesa-license.rst), built with PS5_Vulkan's recipe, which with its native link
  recipe and the PS5 Platform library from the PS5_PayloadSDK fork is GPL-3.0-or-later (PS5_Vulkan-LICENSE.txt,
  PS5_PayloadSDK-LICENSE.txt). The combined executable is therefore subject to the GPL-3.0-or-later.
